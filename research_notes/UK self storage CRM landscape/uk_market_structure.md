# UK Self-Storage Market Structure and Management Software

Research date: 2026-10-14. Note: WebFetch was unavailable (DNS failures), so all findings come from WebSearch result summaries, not full-page reads. Primary reports (SSA UK 2025 PDF, Stora/Janus reports) were NOT read directly; verify headline numbers before quoting.

## 1. Number of UK facilities, operators, and large-chain vs independent split

### Takeaway
SSA UK's latest (2025 report, 2024 data) counts about 1,780 internal self-storage stores (1,127 "significant") run by about 1,480 operators. Including external/container sites the total is about 2,200+ and rising. No source I could reach gives a direct chain vs independent percentage; operator-to-store ratio implies a very fragmented base.

### Cited Findings
- SSA UK Annual Industry Report 2025 was published 30 April 2025 (18th survey year, 10th with Cushman & Wakefield) — [SSA UK](https://ssauk.com/resource/self-storage-annual-report-2025.html)
- Summary of the latest SSA figures: 1,780 internal stores, of which 1,127 are "significant" (over 100 units, British Standard compliant); 1,480 individual operators; 64m sq ft; small container sites growing, especially rural — reported via search summary of SSA UK material, [SSA UK asset](https://ssauk.com/asset/D99288DC%2DDF36%2D4DE1%2DAFEC651DE469F352) (summary only; page not directly read)
- SSA/Cushman 2024 data: floorspace up 7.2% to 64.3m sq ft; occupancy down 1pt to 75.1%; turnover £1.2bn — [SSA UK 2025 report page](https://ssauk.com/publications/uk-annual-industry-report-2025.html) (figures via search summary)
- 2023 report estimated about 2,231 stores incl. about 739 predominantly external (broader definition, not comparable with 1,780) — [SSA UK asset](https://ssauk.com/asset/D99288DC%2DDF36%2D4DE1%2DAFEC651DE469F352)
- History (stale): 830 facilities (2013), 975 facilities among 420 operators (2014), about 1,505 (2018) — [Inside Self Storage 2018](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/associations/uk-self-storage-association-releases-2018-industry-report). SSA said growth partly reflects better capture of single-site and container operators.
- Container sector (CSTA census, mid-2025): 652 operators, 1,212 sites, 111,500 containers; most sites report 90-99% utilisation — [The Mover, 11 Sep 2025](https://www.themover.co.uk/news/2025/09/11/uk-container-self-storage-growing-strong-in-a-competitive-market)
- Big Yellow: 113 stores, 12-site pipeline (Q1 trading statement, 20 Jul 2026); about 75% of revenue from London and commuter towns — [Big Yellow Q1 statement](https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/BYG/q1-trading-statement-z46k3mf0mxxhovl.html), [Big Yellow AR 2025](https://corporate.bigyellow.co.uk/investors/annual-report-2025)
- Safestore H1 FY26 (to 30 Apr 2026): UK revenue £83.9m (+3.3%), UK LFL +2.4%; group closing occupancy 77.5% of CLA at Q3 update 3 Sep 2026 (down 0.9ppt, group incl. France/Spain/Belgium) — [Safestore interim results](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/safestore-holdings--safe/interim-results-/9612339)
- Storage King UK is owned by South African REIT Stor-Age (97.3% acquired 2017) — [Inside Self Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/africa/south-africa-self-storage-operator-stor-age-enters-uk-market-storage-king-acquisition)

### Inferences
- 1,480 operators / 1,780 internal stores implies about 1.2 stores per operator on average: heavy tail of single-site owners. The listed REITs (Big Yellow about 113, Safestore roughly 120-130 UK, Shurgard UK small, Lok'nStore about 40, Access, Storage King and Armadillo-type mid-size chains) probably account for roughly 400-500 stores, i.e. about 25-30% of internal stores (ESTIMATE, low-medium confidence; store counts for the other chains not verified).
- Therefore roughly 1,100-1,300 internal stores and 1,300+ operators sit outside the large listed chains, plus about 650 container operators (CSTA).

### Gaps
- No direct SSA chain/independent split found; Knight Frank returned nothing; Savills/Cushman pages found only as qualitative summaries.
- Safestore, Shurgard, Lok'nStore, Access, Storage King current UK store counts not retrieved.

## 2. Management software used by UK major and mid-size operators

### Takeaway
I found NO public evidence naming the software of Big Yellow, Safestore, Shurgard, Lok'nStore, Access, Storage King, Storage Giant, etc. Large listed chains are widely assumed (not evidenced here) to use bespoke or heavily customised systems. Evidenced UK vendors: Stora, Storman, SiteLink/Storable, Storeganise, Easy Storage Solutions, Unit Trac, 6Storage, Kinnovis, Self Storage Manager.

### Cited Findings
- Stora (Belfast) describes itself as powering more than 25% of UK self-storage facilities (vendor claim) — [Stora LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/storasoftware); Stora 2025 industry report says its platform data covers about 25% of UK facilities — [Stora report](https://www.stora.co/industry-report)
- Norbreck Self Storage (UK) is a Stora customer — [Stora case study](https://www.stora.co/case-studies/how-norbreck-self-storage-built-a-data-led-operation-with-stora)
- Storman (Australasian, UK office Ipswich) lists UK/other customers incl. StorMate, Melcon Storage, Storhub, Storage North; Safestore not listed — [Storman](https://storman.com/), [ISS Buyer's Guide](https://buyersguide.insideselfstorage.com/guides/default.aspx?li=68906). 1st Self Access Storage moved to Storman Cloud in 2021 — [Storman case study](https://storman.com/case-study/customer-case-study-1st-self-access-storage/?lang=en)
- Self Storage Manager (E-SoftSys) UK users: Springfield Self Storage (Cardiff), Andersons Removals Hastings (dated, c.2013+) — [Mini Storage Messenger](https://www.ministoragemessenger.com/blog-post/springfield-self-storage-uk-implements-self-storage-manager-ssm/)
- Capterra UK category lists Easy Storage Solutions (703 reviews, SME focus), Unit Trac (68), 6Storage (90), Kinnovis, Storman (9), Stora (21) — [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/directory/30606/self-storage/software). Review counts are a weak proxy for share.
- SiteLink (Storable) is the global incumbent; pricing quote-only; UK share not found — [competitor-site claim](https://sitelinkcomau.r6d.dev/?p=5589)
- Job adverts (Storage Giant, Surbiton, Edmonton, Pickfords) did not name software — [DWP Find a Job](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/17738950)

### Inferences
- Stora is the most credible UK-native challenger with a stated large footprint; the claim is vendor-sourced and may count sites loosely.
- Big Yellow, Safestore: unknown; do not assert in a report.

### Gaps
- Operator-to-software mapping for all named majors: not evidenced. Next steps: job adverts on operator career sites, Wappalyzer/BuiltWith on booking widgets (DataForSEO technologies tool available), Inside Self Storage UK articles, LinkedIn "Storable"/"SiteLink" UK customer lists, direct vendor calls.
- Storeganise, Storage Vault, Storable UK sites: no UK facts found.

## 4. Independent/small-operator segment and price sensitivity

### Takeaway
Most buyable segment: about 1,100-1,300 independent internal stores plus about 650 container operators (about 1,200 sites). Tools sell for roughly £25-£300 per month per site.

### Cited Findings
- Stora tiers listed at about £99 / £149 / £299 per month by aggregators; dynamic pricing in mid tier; 50-unit minimum claimed by competitor (unverified) — [Capterra Stora](https://capterra.com/p/228462/Stora/), [FrontDesk review](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/self-storage-software/stora/)
- Storeganise from about $90/month; REDA about $95/month; Rentya $25/month up to 50 units — [Capterra Storeganise](https://www.capterra.com/p/232660/Storeganise), [Rentya](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1079300/Rentya)
- Stora offers financing (Stora Capital, UK only) — [Stora Capital](https://stora.co/capital)
- Claim that independents mostly price annually in spreadsheets and an 8-12% revenue gap from algorithmic pricing: US-oriented startup page, unverified — [liveinthefuture.org](https://liveinthefuture.org/startups/self-storage-dynamic-pricing-saas.html)

### Inferences
- Add-on analytics priced above about £50-100/month per site will meet resistance when core PMS costs £99-£299; per-operator multi-site pricing or % of uplift is likelier to work. Addressable (SAM) rough: about 2,500-3,000 sites x £600-£1,800/yr = about £1.5m-£5m ARR ceiling for small operators (ESTIMATE).

### Gaps
- No UK willingness-to-pay data.

## 5. Trends

### Takeaway
Supply growing faster than demand: occupancy softened to about 75%; operators report stable occupancy and rental growth in 2025; revenue management is now cited as the differentiator.

### Cited Findings
- Occupancy 75.1% (2024 data, -1pt), floorspace +7.2% — [SSA UK](https://ssauk.com/publications/uk-annual-industry-report-2025.html)
- Savills: broadly stable UK occupancy and rental growth H1 2025; subdued transactions H2 2025, recovery expected 2026 — [Savills](https://www.savills.co.uk/research_articles/242629/380476-0), [Savills](https://www.savills.com/research_articles/255800/386935-0)
- Cushman & Wakefield update (Aug 2026): professional operators grow rents through pricing discipline; weaker operators discount; revenue management and scale separate the strongest — [SSA UK slides](https://ssauk.com/resource/uk-annual-industry-report-update-slides.html)
- Stora: indoor occupancy peaks 77% in October; nearly 70% of operators favour transparent pricing — [Stora report](https://stora.co/industry-report-2025)
- Safestore pipeline 225k sq ft in H2 2026 and 733k sq ft FY27+; Big Yellow pipeline 12 stores — sources above.

### Inferences
- Consolidation is ongoing via REIT/PE buying (Storage King/Stor-Age precedent) but specific 2025-26 deals not verified.

### Gaps
- Revenue management adoption percentage in UK: not found. 2025-26 M&A list: not found.
