# Competitive landscape: self-storage occupancy analytics, benchmarking, dashboards and revenue management (UK/Europe focus)

Method note: research date 2026-10-14. WebFetch failed (DNS errors) for every page I tried, so all findings come from web-search result summaries, which cite vendor sites, trade press (Inside Self-Storage, Modern Storage Media) and directories (Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice). Nearly everything is vendor-sourced. I found no pricing for most specialist products, and I found no first-hand operator complaint threads (Reddit/Facebook/LinkedIn/forums), because none surfaced in searches and I could not browse those platforms.

## Which products offer occupancy reporting, economic occupancy, rate/revenue management, benchmarking or BI for self storage?

### Takeaway
The market splits into four layers: (1) built-in reporting inside each management system (Storable/SiteLink/storEDGE, Stora, Storeganise, Kinnovis, Self Storage Manager); (2) third-party revenue-management engines (Prorize, Veritec) that bolt onto specific systems; (3) market/competitor rate-and-supply intelligence (StorTrack, Radius+, Aggregate Intelligence); (4) generic BI (Power BI, Zoho Analytics) or portfolio dashboards. I found no product that is an independent, vendor-agnostic occupancy and economic-occupancy dashboard with peer benchmarking for UK operators.

### Cited Findings

**Built-in reporting in management systems**
- Storable launched "Business Intelligence" on 22 Oct 2025 for Storable Edge operators (revenue, occupancy, marketing performance). It was available for Edge customers, with SiteLink support "coming in the future". It includes 13 free management reports, growth reports, revenue-management performance reports and an optional report builder; scheduled delivery and interactive filters are listed. Further BI features were planned through 2026 including an "Ask Your Data" feature in Edge. Tiering and pricing were not found. — [Storable BI launch, Modern Storage Media](https://www.modernstoragemedia.com/news/storable-launches-business-intelligence-reporting-solution); [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/storable-launches-business-intelligence-analytics-platform-for-the-self-storage-industry); [Storable BI page](https://www.storable.com/business-intelligence/)
- Storable's launch release described operators relying on "a patchwork of spreadsheets" and lacking reporting confidence (as paraphrased in search summary). — [Webull syndication of release](https://www.webull.com/news/13726029011161088)
- storEDGE released integrated business-intelligence tools around 2020 pulling software, website, operations and marketing data into one warehouse, in response to owners frustrated by fragmented data across locations (as reported in the summary of the trade article). — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/self-storage-technology-firm-storedge-releases-integrated-business-intelligence-tools)
- SiteLink marketing claims 15 management reports plus 11 unit reports including occupancy, move-ins and move-outs (vendor page, a SiteLink Australia mirror). — [SiteLink reporting page](https://mail.sitelinkcomau.r6d.dev/self-storage-reporting/)
- Storeganise: optional Analytics add-on (must be requested from support) with pre-built reports for occupancy (by units or area; available/blocked/occupied/reserved), move-ins/outs, revenue and ageing; by-site comparison; filters; data refreshed once daily. Zoho Analytics integration for custom reports/export. — [Storeganise help centre](https://help.storeganise.com/article/423-introduction-to-the-analytics-dashboard); [Storeganise blog](https://storeganise.com/blog/analytics-and-reports-for-self-storage); [Zoho partner page](https://storeganise.com/partner/zoho-analytics)
- Storeganise says it has 1,200+ facilities in 45+ countries, open API (search-result summary of vendor/SSA UK listing). — [SSA UK supplier listing](https://ssauk.com/supplier/storeganise-ltd.html)
- Storeganise also publishes content on operators using ChatGPT/Claude to analyse their own exported data, an indicator that native reporting is not seen as sufficient. — [Storeganise blog](https://storeganise.com/blog/self-storage-data-analysis-ai)
- Stora (Belfast-based): Capterra/GetApp list "starting from £99" usage-based pricing with no tier breakdown; one 2022 Software Advice reviewer said reporting was limited compared with larger products (single dated review). — [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1011583/stora); [Capterra UK reviews](https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/1011583/stora); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.co.uk/software/210892/stora)
- Kinnovis (European): says it tracks occupancy, rent roll, RevPAU, delinquency, move-ins/outs in real time; Capterra lists an analytics dashboard; has a direct Power BI connection showing occupancy, revenue and collections across sites (Power BI Pro/Premium licence needed to publish). Pricing is custom quote only. No named "economic occupancy" metric found. Claims 17 European countries. — [Kinnovis metrics blog](https://kinnovis.com/10-essential-self-storage-metrics-every-operator-should-track/); [Kinnovis x Power BI](https://kinnovis.com/kinnovis-x-power-bi/); [Kinnovis pricing](https://kinnovis.com/pricing/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/property/kinnovis-profile/)
- Self Storage Manager (E-SoftSys, UK presence e.g. Springfield Self Storage UK): Power BI dashboards on Microsoft Fabric, 50+ dashboards, aimed at large operators/REITs, can integrate external data (accounting, HR, call centre, revenue management, Google Analytics). — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/self-storage-manager-software-integrates-power-bi-dashboards-and-reports-fueled-by-microsoft-fabric); [Springfield UK](https://modernstoragemedia.com/blog-post/springfield-self-storage-uk-implements-self-storage-manager-ssm)
- Other UK-listed management systems on Capterra: Storman (4.4/5, 9 reviews), Unit Trac, 6Storage, Kinnovis, Easy Storage Solutions. — [Capterra UK directory](https://www.capterra.co.uk/directory/30606/self-storage/software)
- Rentya (European platform) lists pricing management and reporting among features. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1079300/Rentya)

**Revenue management engines**
- Prorize: AI revenue management ("Self Storage Revenue Optimizer"); claims 90+ operators in 28 countries (Storeganise partner page; Storable's page says "over a dozen countries", so the count conflicts, both vendor-sourced). Integrates with SiteLink and Storeganise. Case study: Green Storage (Sweden), over 60,000 units, >50% revenue growth claimed. Reported 60+ new clients in 2024. No pricing found. — [Storeganise partner page](https://storeganise.com/partner/prorize); [Storable integration](https://storable.com/sitelink-integrations/prorize-llc); [Modern Storage Media Green Storage](https://www.modernstoragemedia.com/news/prorize-helps-green-storage-transform-pricing-across-60000-units); [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/self-storage-revenue-management-technology-provider-prorize-reports-3rd-year-of-record-breaking-growth)
- Veritec: pricing analytics for SiteLink and storEDGE data via a custom nightly-sync API; unit-level price recommendations and per-lease rent-increase suggestions; claims 4-9% annual revenue uplift. No UK/European presence found. — [Storable Veritec integration](https://www.storable.com/?p=12639)

**Market / competitor intelligence and benchmarks**
- StorTrack Explorer expanded to the UK and 32 European countries in Aug 2024: 12,000+ facilities, seven years of history, development projects; three-tier subscription (free, basic, upgraded). Earlier UK "MarketView" (2020) sold national unlimited or pay-per-market. No prices found. — [Inside Self-Storage Explorer](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/stortrack-expands-explorer-self-storage-market-analysis-platform-to-cover-europe-uk); [MarketView](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/stortrack-launches-marketview-platform-for-the-uk-self-storage-market)
- Aggregate Intelligence's StorageRatesAPI: rate data for US, Canada and Europe; claims over 90% of top operators use it (vendor claim). — [Aggregate Intelligence](https://aggregateintelligence.com/?p=4869)
- Radius+ (US): supply/saturation, unit-level pricing refreshed daily across roughly 48,000 facilities, Excel add-in (Relay). US-centred; a 2018 report says occupancy tracking was restricted to data-sharing operators; I could not confirm it is still offered. — [Radius+ features](https://radiusplus.com/features); [Inside Self-Storage 2018](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliersvendors/union-realtime-partners-andover-properties-launch-self-storage-facility-occupancy)
- The Storage Scanner: pan-European comparison/marketplace with provider analytics/monetisation; no evidence of pooled operator KPIs. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/the-storage-scanner/discuss)
- Stora's annual UK Industry Insights (2023, 2024, 2025): built from customer data; 2024 sample nearly 20% of UK facilities; 2025 says 580+ UK sites and 25% of UK facilities; monthly occupancy patterns, online rental share (62% to 70% 2023 to 2024). Vendor-reported. — [Stora 2025 report](https://stora.co/industry-report-2025); [Inside Self-Storage on 2024 report](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/software-firm-stora-releases-uk-self-storage-industry-insights-2024-report)
- SSA UK annual survey (with Cushman & Wakefield): the 16th edition (2023 report) covers 2022 data; earlier editions report average occupancy 71% (2014) and 73% (2016); a secondary summary gives 77.5% for the 2024 report. StorTrack-sourced secondary figure of about 79% UK occupancy. FEDESSA European survey gives pan-European averages (about 78% excluding UK in its first edition; a later summary says 79.8% to 81.5%, year unclear). These are annual, survey-based, not live KPI benchmarking. — [Inside Self-Storage SSA UK](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/self-storage-industry-news/ssa-uk-releases-results-of-annual-self-storage-survey-report); [Neighbor blog](https://www.neighbor.com/storage-blog/?p=16645); [Stora FEDESSA post](https://stora.co/blog/fedessa-european-annual-industry-report)

**Portfolio dashboards / generic BI**
- White Label Storage launched a limited-beta portfolio analytics dashboard (Feb 2026) combining Google Ads, storEDGE, SpareFoot, Zendesk and other sources into one facility-level view. This is US/storEDGE-centric. — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/white-label-storage-launches-self-storage-portfolio-analytics-dashboard-in-limited-beta-release)
- OpenTech Alliance INSOMNIAC StoreTracker updated to simplify portfolio operations workflows (US). — [OpenTech Alliance](https://opentechalliance.com/blog/storetracker-redesign-simplifies-self-storage-operations/)
- Tenant Inc. white paper notes BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau can turn raw data into insights (generic). — [Tenant Inc](https://www.tenantinc.com/self-storage-data-into-profits)

### Inferences
- The only vendors with multi-site dashboards tie them to their own CRM (Storable BI on Edge first, SiteLink later; Kinnovis and SSM Power BI; Storeganise Analytics/Zoho). A multi-CRM operator would need to stitch several.
- Revenue management (Prorize, Veritec) is a different job from occupancy reporting; these are potential partners or data consumers rather than direct competitors.
- StorTrack/Radius+ occupy the external-market-data layer, and are complementary to internal-KPI aggregation.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of "Yardi-style", Cubby or "Opendoor-style" benchmark products for self-storage; "Self Storage Data" as a named product was not found. Not checked further.
- Economic occupancy as a named metric: only a general operator guide mentioned it; no product page confirmed it for any UK-relevant vendor.
- Specialist self-storage data agencies/consultants: none identified.

## Are any multi-CRM / vendor-agnostic, and how do they ingest data?

### Takeaway
No fully vendor-agnostic occupancy-intelligence product for UK operators was found. The nearest are integration-led (Prorize works with SiteLink and Storeganise; Veritec with SiteLink and storEDGE) and generic BI tools with connectors.

### Cited Findings
- Prorize integrates with at least SiteLink and Storeganise. — [Storable](https://storable.com/sitelink-integrations/prorize-llc); [Storeganise](https://storeganise.com/partner/prorize)
- Veritec syncs from SiteLink and storEDGE via a custom nightly API. — [Storable](https://www.storable.com/?p=12639)
- Storeganise exposes an open API and a Zoho Analytics sync. — [Storeganise](https://storeganise.com/partner/zoho-analytics)
- SSM's Fabric workspace can ingest external systems (accounting, HR, call centre, revenue management, Google Analytics). — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/self-storage-manager-software-integrates-power-bi-dashboards-and-reports-fueled-by-microsoft-fabric)
- White Label Storage's dashboard combines multiple marketing/ops sources but is storEDGE-centric. — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/white-label-storage-launches-self-storage-portfolio-analytics-dashboard-in-limited-beta-release)
- Radius+ Relay gives data access inside Excel (market data, not operator CRM data). — [Radius+](https://radiusplus.com/features)

### Inferences
- A true multi-CRM layer for UK (Stora, Storeganise, Storman, SiteLink, Kinnovis, SSM, Easy Storage etc.) appears to be unoccupied white space, but absence of evidence from limited searches is not proof; confirm with direct vendor and operator checks.
- Ingestion would likely have to be API where available (Storeganise, Kinnovis), scheduled report export/CSV for others; I did not verify API availability for Stora, Storman or SiteLink UK.

### Gaps
- API/export capabilities of Stora, Storman, Space Manager, Easy Storage, SiteLink (UK) not verified.

## Pricing models and indicative prices

### Takeaway
Pricing is largely undisclosed; custom quote or bundled with the CRM is the norm. Only Stora's "from £99" listing is a concrete figure.

### Cited Findings
- Stora: from £99, usage-based (Capterra/GetApp listing). — [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1011583/stora)
- Kinnovis: custom quote; a third-party site estimates $70-$150/month for standard facilities (benchmark estimate, not published). — [Kinnovis pricing](https://kinnovis.com/pricing/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/property/kinnovis-profile/)
- Storable BI: some reports free in Edge; tiered with optional report builder; prices not found. — [Modern Storage Media](https://www.modernstoragemedia.com/news/storable-launches-business-intelligence-reporting-solution)
- StorTrack Explorer: free/basic/upgraded tiers; MarketView national subscription or pay-per-market; prices not found. — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/stortrack-expands-explorer-self-storage-market-analysis-platform-to-cover-europe-uk)
- Storeganise Analytics: optional add-on (price not found). — [Storeganise help](https://help.storeganise.com/article/423-introduction-to-the-analytics-dashboard)
- Prorize: no public pricing found. — [Storeganise partner](https://storeganise.com/partner/prorize)
- A competitor's (biased) Product Hunt post says SiteLink/Storable pricing is opaque (quote-based). — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/storagezen/picking-self-storage-software-in-the-uk-what-nobody-tells-you-about-sitelink-stora-and-pricing)

### Inferences
- A standalone analytics SaaS could be priced per site per month; the only anchor is Stora's £99 base for the whole CRM, implying a standalone tool must be cheap per site or sold per portfolio.

### Gaps
- Prices for Prorize, Veritec, StorTrack, Radius+, Storable BI, Storeganise Analytics unknown. Need direct quotes.

## Strengths, gaps and UK relevance

### Takeaway
UK relevance is strongest for Stora (about 25% of UK sites, vendor-claimed), Storeganise, StorTrack Explorer and SSM/Kinnovis. US tools are mostly not UK-focused.

### Cited Findings
- Stora claims 25% of UK facilities and runs a UK benchmark report. — [Stora](https://stora.co/industry-report-2025)
- StorTrack covers UK and 32 European countries. — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/stortrack-expands-explorer-self-storage-market-analysis-platform-to-cover-europe-uk)
- StorTrack's 2025 Europe update says dynamic pricing and analytics are increasingly used in the UK, France, Germany and Spain. — [Storable/StorTrack search result](https://stora.co/blog/growth-in-europe-self-storage-market) (as reported in search summary; the update itself not opened)
- Storeganise Analytics limitation: daily refresh only, add-on must be requested. — [Storeganise help](https://help.storeganise.com/article/423-introduction-to-the-analytics-dashboard)
- Storable BI available first for Edge, SiteLink later (a key limitation for SiteLink-dependent UK operators). — [Modern Storage Media](https://www.modernstoragemedia.com/news/storable-launches-business-intelligence-reporting-solution)

### Inferences
- Benchmarks available to UK operators are annual surveys (SSA UK/FEDESSA) or vendor-customer samples (Stora), with no live, cross-CRM peer comparison.
- Stora's customer-base benchmark is a source of bias (only its own customers) and a potential competitive response.

### Gaps
- Actual SiteLink/Storable market share in UK not found; no independent adoption data.

## Evidence of demand: operator complaints about reporting, forum/social threads, reviews

### Takeaway
Demand evidence is indirect: vendors repeatedly describe operators drowning in spreadsheets and fragmented data. I found no direct operator posts or surveys.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor statements (Storable, storEDGE, White Label Storage) that operators patch together spreadsheets or fragmented data (see above). — [Webull](https://www.webull.com/news/13726029011161088); [Inside Self-Storage storEDGE](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/self-storage-technology-firm-storedge-releases-integrated-business-intelligence-tools); [White Label Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/suppliers-products/white-label-storage-launches-self-storage-portfolio-analytics-dashboard-in-limited-beta-release)
- A Self Storage Manager comparison page flagged limited BI/multi-location analysis (sales-side vendor-comparison, not operator testimony). — [Fitgap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/self-storage-manager)
- One 2022 reviewer said Stora reporting was limited. — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.co.uk/software/210892/stora)
- Inside Self-Storage has an article on getting more from software reports. — [Inside Self-Storage](https://www.insideselfstorage.com/software/harnessing-the-power-of-your-self-storage-software-reports)
- Large vendors investing in BI (Storable 2025, SSM/Fabric, Kinnovis Power BI) and ChatGPT/Claude data-analysis content from Storeganise suggest demand. — [Storeganise blog](https://storeganise.com/blog/self-storage-data-analysis-ai)

### Inferences
- Several vendors shipping BI in 2025-26 suggests operator demand is real, but also that CRMs are closing the gap within their own walled gardens; the multi-CRM niche matters mainly for operators or investors with mixed systems after acquisitions, which is plausible but not evidenced.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Facebook group, LinkedIn or forum threads found; G2/Capterra reviews mentioning reporting limited to one Stora review. Needs manual browsing of SSA UK/FEDESSA events, LinkedIn and Facebook groups, and G2/Capterra reviews of SiteLink, Storman and Easy Storage.
- No survey quantifying operator dissatisfaction with reporting.
